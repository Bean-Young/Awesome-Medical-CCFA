Read the methodology ↗
Which conferences are included?

We apply the 58 A-ranked conferences in the CCF 2022 sixth edition retrospectively to 2021–2025. This is a fixed cohort, not historical year-specific rankings. ECCV, EMNLP, MICCAI, and ICLR are not included in this cohort. Only eligible main-conference full papers are intended for inclusion; ACL collection is currently limited to main-track long papers.

What counts as medical research?

Records address medicine, clinical care, biomedical data, human health, or directly relevant biological applications. Search terms identify candidates; relevance review and source provenance determine inclusion. Screening methods vary by source and are recorded in the coverage ledger. This catalogue is not yet exhaustive.

How should I interpret the statistics?

All charts describe the currently indexed records. Venue-year ratios require a matching full-paper denominator; no denominator means no ratio. Topic tags overlap. Citation counts are snapshots from the recorded source and date, not a quality score. Missing values remain unknown.

Where do figures and affiliations come from?

Paper figures are extracted from original papers only when available, with source attribution. No generated illustration is used as a paper figure. Institutions are shown only when a record has supporting source data. Read original abstracts on the linked publisher pages. Concise introductions are title-derived descriptions.
